Profile

I turn manual, leaky processes into systems that run themselves.

Most businesses lose deals in the gaps — a lead that waited 3 hours for a reply, a missed call that never got a text back, a quote that sat in a drafts folder.

I close those gaps. Working across GoHighLevel, n8n, Make, and Zapier, I design automations and AI agents that respond instantly, qualify on their own, book straight into the calendar, and keep the CRM clean — so the team wakes up to booked appointments, not a backlog.

My background is enterprise-grade: 10+ years in automation — from RPA and mainframe at Accenture (American Express, Sempra Energy) to RPA Technical Lead at FPT Software (UNIQLO, Canon, Far Eastern Organization) and Automation Head at GCash. Along the way I've delivered 100+ production bots across enterprise clients — 90+ at Sempra Energy alone at 99% SLA — and 30–50% efficiency gains.

Today that same discipline goes into AI agents and no-code automation — multi-tenant AI front desks on GoHighLevel, Twilio voice agents that book by phone, and 13+ hosted n8n workflows. Systems that actually ship and hold up under real traffic, not demos.
